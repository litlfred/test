Vaccination of pregnant women

Measles vaccines: WHO position paper – April 2017, journal pp. 225 · 1 recommendation(s), 2 remark(s). Every statement and remark is verbatim from the English column of the PDF; the build fails if it is not found on the page it cites.

R34

As a precautionary measure, measles vaccine – alone or in combination with other vaccines – should be avoided during pregnancy.

journal p.225 · PDF page 21 · WER9217-R34

Remark R34-1 · journal p.225 · PDF page 21

Inadvertent administration of measles vaccine during pregnancy is not a reason for terminating the pregnancy.

Remark R34-2 · journal p.218 · PDF page 14

Although measles virus has not been shown to have any teratogenic effect, because measles vaccine is a live attenuated vaccine and may pose a theoretical risk to the developing fetus, in particular due to fever that may result from vaccination, as a precautionary measure it is not recommended for pregnant women.
