Vaccination of health-care workers

Measles vaccines: WHO position paper – April 2017, journal pp. 225 · 3 recommendation(s), 2 remark(s). Every statement and remark is verbatim from the English column of the PDF; the build fails if it is not found on the page it cites.

R35

Because of the known risk of spreading measles from HCWs to patients or from patients to HCWs, all HCWs and any staff who are in contact with patients should be immune to measles.

journal p.225 · PDF page 21 · WER9217-R35

Remark R35-1 · journal p.225 · PDF page 21

All persons involved in patient care such as health care professionals, residents, students, laboratory staff, as well as persons in public health such as field workers, epidemiologists, laboratory staff and community health-care workers.

Remark R35-2 · journal p.225 · PDF page 21

Either written documentation of receipt of 2 doses of MCV and at least 1 dose of RCV or positive serologic (IgG) test results from a proficient laboratory.

R36

Verification of vaccination and/or history of measles should be integrated into standard infection control guidelines or other standards of care for health-care workers.

journal p.225 · PDF page 21 · WER9217-R36

R37

For HCWs who have contact with patients, documentation of immunity should be required before signing an employment contract or entering into a training programme.

journal p.225 · PDF page 21 · WER9217-R37
