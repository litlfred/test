Measles vaccination of HIV-infected individuals

Measles vaccines: WHO position paper – April 2017, journal pp. 225–226 · 7 recommendation(s), 1 remark(s). Every statement and remark is verbatim from the English column of the PDF; the build fails if it is not found on the page it cites.

R39

Given the severe course of measles in patients with AIDS, measles vaccination should be routinely administered to potentially susceptible, asymptomatic HIV-infected children and adults.

journal p.225 · PDF page 21 · WER9217-R39

R40

Vaccination may even be considered for those with symptomatic HIV infection if they are not severely immunosuppressed according to conventional definitions.

journal p.225 · PDF page 21 · refines R39 · WER9217-R40

R41

In areas where there is a high incidence of both HIV infection and measles, an initial dose of MCV may be offered as early as age 6 months (recorded as MCV0). The 2 routine doses of MCV (MCV1 and MCV2) should then be administered to these children according to the national immunization schedule.

Condition: In areas where there is a high incidence of both HIV infection and measles

R42

An additional dose of MCV should be administered to HIV-infected children receiving HAART following immune reconstitution.

journal p.226 · PDF page 22 · WER9217-R42

Remark R42-1 · journal p.226 · PDF page 22

Current evidence is insufficient to recommend an additional dose for children who start HAART prior to the first dose of MCV.

R43

If CD4+ T lymphocyte counts are monitored, an additional dose of MCV should be administered when immune reconstitution has been achieved, e.g. when the CD4+ T lymphocyte count reaches 20–25%.

Condition: If CD4+ T lymphocyte counts are monitored

journal p.226 · PDF page 22 · refines R42 · WER9217-R43

R44

Where CD4+ T lymphocyte monitoring is not available, children should receive an additional dose of MCV 6–12 months after initiation of HAART.

Condition: Where CD4+ T lymphocyte monitoring is not available

journal p.226 · PDF page 22 · refines R42 · WER9217-R44

R45

A supplementary dose of MCV (recorded as MCV0) should be considered for infants known to be exposed (i.e. born to an HIV-infected woman) or soon after diagnosis of HIV infection in children older than 6 months who are not receiving HAART and for whom the risk of measles is high, with the aim of providing partial protection until they are revaccinated after immune reconstitution with HAART.
